Who We Are

About the Edge AI Lab

Bringing intelligence closer to data, devices, and decisions.

Edge AI Lab is an independent research laboratory focused on the design and development of intelligent computing systems that integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) with Edge Computing. The lab operates independently and is not affiliated with or part of any university, department, or other institution. By moving computation and decision-making closer to data sources, we aim to reduce latency, optimize network resources, improve responsiveness, and support reliable intelligent services.

Our research explores how distributed edge infrastructure, learning algorithms, connected devices, and intelligent control mechanisms can work together to address the growing computational demands of smart roads, connected infrastructure, and other cyber-physical environments.

Our vision is to create intelligent, autonomous, and sustainable edge-computing environments that enable timely decisions wherever they are needed.
Our VisionEnable next-generation intelligent systems through edge-centric AI.
Our MissionDevelop efficient, secure, and adaptive AI-enabled edge solutions.
Our FocusLow latency, resource efficiency, scalability, and trustworthy intelligence.
Our ApproachResearch, simulation, prototyping, experimentation, and collaboration.
